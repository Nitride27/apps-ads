Big Letter Word Search

Privacy policy

Applies to the Android app Big Letter Word Search (com.bigletterwords.app), listed on Google Play as "Large Print Word Search: Easy".

What stays on your phone

Your progress, streak, settings and any puzzles you make are saved on your device only. There are no accounts, and the app has no servers of its own, so none of this is sent to us.

Reminders

If you turn on the morning reminder, the notification is scheduled on your phone. No data is sent anywhere to deliver it.

Advertising

The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and AdMob may fill some of them through Unity Ads. These services may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address, general device information and ad interactions in order to show, measure and limit ads, and to personalise ads where you have allowed it. In the UK, EEA and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change it at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your phone's settings.

Read how Google uses data from apps that use its services and Unity's privacy policy.

Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Use the developer email on the app's Google Play listing.
