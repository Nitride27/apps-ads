Friendsgiving potluck list

For 10 to 12 people, assign one turkey (or a ham or vegetarian main), five or six sides, two pies, a starter, drinks and the plates and ice. Put the list on a sign-up sheet so each dish is claimed once.

Step by step

  1. Start from the Friendsgiving template. It has 18 items ready: turkey, mashed potatoes, stuffing, gravy, green beans, sweet potatoes, mac and cheese, cranberry sauce, rolls, salad, pies, drinks and supplies.
  2. Trim or add. Remove what you are making yourself, and set how many people each item needs, like two for wine.
  3. Share one link. Guests tap it, see what is still open and pick a dish on any phone, no app needed.

The host usually makes the turkey, because it needs the oven longest. Ask guests to bring sides ready to serve or needing only a short reheat.

Questions

What should the host make for Friendsgiving?

The turkey or main, and the gravy if it uses the drippings. Everything else travels well and can be assigned.

How do I stop people bringing the same dish?

Use a sign-up sheet where each item can be claimed once. Guests see what is taken before they choose.
