Printable potluck sign-up sheet

Make your sheet in the app and tap Printable sheet. You get a PDF on A4 or Letter with every item, who is bringing it, blank lines for open items and a QR code that opens the sheet on a phone.

Get it on Google PlayMake a sheet online

Step by step

  1. Make the sheet. Pick a template and fill in the date and place.
  2. Tap Printable sheet. The app makes the PDF, sized for your country's paper.
  3. Print or share it. Put it on the fridge, the office kitchen or the church foyer.

Paper sheets get messy when two people write on the same line. The QR code lets people sign up on their phone instead, so the host's list stays the one true copy.

Questions

Can I print a potluck sign-up sheet for free?

Yes. The app is free and makes the PDF on your phone. Nothing is uploaded.
