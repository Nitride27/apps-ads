Allergy-friendly potluck

Ask guests to tag their dish when they sign up (vegan, gluten-free, nut-free, dairy-free, halal, kosher, contains alcohol), label it on the table, and give every dish its own spoon. The host then sees how many safe options each guest has.

Get it on Google PlayMake a sheet online

In Potluck Planner each dish carries dietary tags, and the host's sheet sums them up, like "3 vegan · 2 gluten-free". Guests can also leave a note such as "allergic to nuts".

Tags are what guests tell you, not a guarantee. Anyone with a serious allergy should check with the cook.

Questions

How do you label food at a potluck?

A small card by each dish with its name and main allergens: nuts, gluten, dairy, eggs, shellfish, and alcohol.
