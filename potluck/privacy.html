Privacy Policy – Potluck Planner: Sign Up Sheet

Package com.samridha.potluck

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Samridha Shrestha ("we") built the Potluck Planner: Sign Up Sheet app ("the app") and its web sign-up pages. This policy explains what information is handled when you use them. There are no accounts and no server of our own.

Information you enter

Potluck names, dishes (with optional servings, kitchen needs and recipe links), guest names, RSVPs, dates, locations and notes you type into the app are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

Invite and reply links

To invite guests, the app puts the sign-up sheet into a link and hands it to the app you pick (for example SMS, WhatsApp or email); that app's own privacy policy then applies. The sheet sits after the "#" in the link, which browsers never send to a web server, so our pages are only loaded, not told what is in the sheet. A guest's page builds their reply link on their own phone in the same way. The links are compressed, not encrypted: anyone with a link can read the sheet, so share it only with your guests.

The web pages store nothing, except that the web host page may remember your sheets in your own browser's storage so you can find them again.

Maps and recipe links

If you tap "Directions" or a recipe link, the place name or link is handed to a maps app or browser on your device, whose own privacy policy then applies. Nothing is opened unless you tap it, and only web links are ever opened.

Reminders

If you turn on reminders, they are scheduled on your phone by Android. No reminder content leaves your device.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and app interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the menu ("Privacy settings"). The web pages show no ads.

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

Internet and network state: to load ads. Notifications and run at startup: to deliver the reminders you turn on, including after the phone restarts. Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above. The app does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

Because your potluck data is stored only on your device, deleting a potluck in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app removes it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy changes with it and the date at the top is updated.
