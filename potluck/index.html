Questions

Do guests need the app to sign up?

No. The invite is a link that opens in any browser, iPhone included. Guests pick a dish there and send a reply link back.

Is Potluck Planner free?

Yes, and the app shows ads. Five extra color themes unlock by watching a short video. The web pages have no ads.

Do I need an account?

No. Neither the host nor the guests sign up or give an email address.

Where is my sign-up sheet stored?

On the host's phone. The sheet also travels inside the invite link, so guests can see it, but there is no server of ours holding it.

Does the sheet update live?

No. Guests see the sheet as it was when the link was sent. When the sheet changes, the app reminds you to send an update, and it flags any dish claimed twice.

Can I host without the app?

Yes. Make a sheet with the maker on this page. The sheet lives in its link, so bookmark it, and paste guests' replies into it.

Can people sign up in person?

Yes. Show the QR code from the app, or print the sheet: it has a QR code too.

How do I stop two people bringing the same dish?

Each item can be claimed once, or as many times as you set. Guests see what is taken, and the host is told if a dish is doubled up.

Can I move my potlucks to a new phone?

Yes. Send yourself a backup message from the menu and paste it on the new phone.
