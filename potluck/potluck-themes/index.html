Potluck themes

A theme makes it easier to choose a dish and keeps the table from becoming five pasta salads. Popular ones are Friendsgiving, taco bar, around the world, brunch, soup and bread, and holiday feasts like Diwali, Eid, Hanukkah and Lunar New Year.

What is a good potluck theme for a big group?

Taco bar or around the world: both scale well and give each person a clear choice.
