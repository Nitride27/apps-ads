Questions

Is there a free alternative to SignUpGenius for a potluck?

Yes. Potluck Planner is free with ads, and neither the host nor guests make an account.

Can I use Google Sheets for a potluck sign-up?

Yes, with an edit link. Two people can overwrite each other's row, and anyone with the link can change anything.
