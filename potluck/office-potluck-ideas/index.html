Office potluck ideas

Pick a theme, share a sign-up sheet so mains, sides and desserts are balanced, ask for dishes that need no oven at work, and have people label allergens. A link everyone can open without an account works best at work.

Get it on Google PlayMake a sheet online

Themes that work at work

  • Taco bar: one person brings tortillas, others fillings and toppings
  • Around the world: each person brings a dish from home
  • Soup and bread for winter, salad bar for summer
  • Breakfast potluck: bagels, fruit, egg bakes
  • Chili cook-off with a vote

Keep it fair

Not everyone can cook. Let people sign up for drinks, plates, napkins or ice. The office template in the app has plates, cutlery and cups as items.

Questions

How do you organize an office potluck?

Set a date, pick a theme, share a sign-up sheet, and send the final who's-bringing-what list a day before.

Do colleagues need to install anything?

No. The sign-up link opens in any browser.
