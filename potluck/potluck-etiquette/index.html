Potluck etiquette

Sign up early, bring what you said you would, ready to serve with its own spoon, and label what is in it. Hosts provide the main, plates and a clear list, so nobody has to guess.

For guests

  • Sign up when the list arrives, not the night before
  • Bring enough for at least half the guests
  • Bring a serving spoon and a dish you don't need back that night
  • Label allergens: nuts, gluten, dairy, and if it contains alcohol
  • Tell the host early if plans change
  • Offer to take a share of the cleanup

For hosts

  • Make the main or the turkey
  • Share one list so dishes are not doubled
  • Say what space you have: oven, fridge, outlets
  • Ask about allergies when people sign up
  • Send the final list before people shop
  • Have containers ready for leftovers

Questions

Is it rude to bring store-bought food to a potluck?

No. A good store-bought dessert or drinks is better than nothing, and many hosts welcome it.

Should you take your leftovers home from a potluck?

Offer them to the host first. If they say take it, do.
