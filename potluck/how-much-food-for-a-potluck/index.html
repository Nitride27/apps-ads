How much food for a potluck

Plan about 1.25 lb (0.6 kg) of whole turkey per person, half a cup of each side, a pie for every six people, two drinks in the first hour and one an hour after, and a pound of ice each. Use the calculator below for your numbers.

These are common rule-of-thumb portions for adults. At a potluck everyone brings something, so most dishes go further: plan sides for about half the guests each.

Thawing a frozen turkey

Thaw in the fridge, allowing about a day for every 4 lb (1.8 kg). A 16 lb turkey needs four days, so it goes in the fridge on Sunday for a Thursday dinner.

Questions

How big a turkey do I need for 10 people?

About 13 lb (6 kg), or 17 lb (8 kg) if you want leftovers.

How many pies for 20 people?

Four pies of eight slices, since not everyone takes pie when there are other desserts.
