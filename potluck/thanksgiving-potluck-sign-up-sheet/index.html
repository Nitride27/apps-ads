Thanksgiving potluck sign-up sheet

Make the sheet from a Thanksgiving template, share its link in your group chat, and guests claim a dish each. Nobody needs an account, and you see in one list what is covered and what is still open.

Get it on Google PlayMake a sheet online

Step by step

  1. Make the sheet. Use the sheet maker on this site or the app. Pick the Friendsgiving template; it covers Thanksgiving too.
  2. Send the link. Paste it into the family or friends chat. It opens on iPhone and Android.
  3. Collect the replies. Each guest sends a reply link back. In the app one tap adds it; on the web, paste it into your sheet.
  4. Send the final list. Share who is bringing what, so everyone knows before they shop.

Thanksgiving 2026 is on Thursday 26 November. Send the sheet at least a week before, so people can plan shopping.

Want it on paper too? The app prints the sheet as a PDF, with a QR code that opens it on any phone.

Questions

Is there a free Thanksgiving sign-up sheet?

Yes. Potluck Planner is free and has no accounts. The web sheet maker on this site works without installing anything.

Does every guest need the app?

No. Guests sign up from a link in the browser. Only an Android host gets the extras: reminders, one-tap replies and the printable sheet.
