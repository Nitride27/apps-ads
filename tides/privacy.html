Privacy policy

Tide Chart & Fishing Times

Android app, package com.tidefishing.app, published on Google Play by MadAxe Studios

Last updated: 18 November 2026

Samridha Shrestha ("we") built the Tide Chart & Fishing Times app ("the app"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use it. There is no account and no sign-up.

Tide predictions and your settings

Tide times, the tide chart, fishing times, sunrise, sunset and moon phases are calculated on your phone from data built into the app. Your chosen station, favorites, units, alert settings and "your level" are stored only on your device.

Location

If you allow it, the app reads your approximate location to find the nearest tide station. The location is used on your phone only and is never uploaded or stored by us. You can say no and search for a town or station instead. The app never asks for precise location.

Live water levels

At stations with a NOAA gauge, the app fetches today's measured water level from our server (tides-api.samridhashrestha.com.np, run on Cloudflare), which passes the request on to NOAA. The request contains only the station number. Like any web request, it also reveals your device's IP address to the server. We use the IP address only to limit how many requests one device can make, and we do not store it; Cloudflare keeps short-lived technical logs. No location or other information is sent.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads using Google AdMob: a small banner, occasional full-screen ads (when the app opens and at natural breaks such as after picking a station), and an optional rewarded video that unlocks 30 days of tides for 24 hours. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location derived from the IP address, and app interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → "Ad privacy choices".

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Tide alerts and the widget

If you turn on tide alerts, Android schedules them on your phone. The home-screen widget reads the tide for your station from the app on your phone. No alert or widget content leaves your device.

Sharing

If you share a day's tides, the app puts the text and a link to our tide website into a message and hands it to the app you pick. That app's own privacy policy then applies. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share.

Permissions

Approximate location: to find the nearest station, as above. Notifications and run at startup: to deliver the tide alerts you turn on, also after the phone restarts. Internet and network state: for ads and live water levels. Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above. The app does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, precise location or files.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

Everything the app keeps is stored on your device. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app removes it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Safety

Tide times are predictions, not measurements, and are not for navigation. See the safety notice in the app.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy changes with it and the date at the top is updated.
