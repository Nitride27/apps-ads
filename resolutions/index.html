Start in seconds

Pick from 30 resolution ideas, like drinking water, reading 20 pages or a no-spend day, or make your own.

Track it your way

A simple check-off, a count (8 glasses) or an amount (20 pages, 10,000 steps). Daily, weekdays, 3 times a week or every few days.

Streaks that forgive

Current and best streaks, a habit strength score, milestones from 3 days to a year, and a streak freeze for the day life gets in the way.

Home-screen widget

See today's habits and tick them off without opening the app. Light and dark.

Reminders on time

Only on days the habit is due, and never for something you already did.

Do it with a friend

Send a challenge by text or WhatsApp. Your friend pastes it in and you both start the same habit.

Questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app and add a habit. There is nothing to sign up for.

Where are my habits stored?

Only on your phone. You can back them up to a message you send yourself and restore them on a new phone, or export everything to a spreadsheet (CSV).

Is it free?

Yes. The app is free and supported by Google AdMob ads. See the privacy policy for what that means.

What if I miss a day?

Use a streak freeze, or fix a forgotten check-in from the history calendar. The habit strength score doesn't drop to zero after one bad day.

Is there an iPhone version?

Not yet. It's Android only for now.
