Privacy Policy – Habit Tracker: Resolution 2027

Android app, package com.giftdraw.resolutions, published by MadAxe Studios

Last updated: 16 November 2026

Samridha Shrestha ("we") built the Habit Tracker: Resolution 2027 app ("the app"), published on Google Play by MadAxe Studios. This policy explains what information is handled when you use it. There is no account and no server of our own.

Information you enter

Your habits, check-ins, counts and amounts, streaks, schedules, reminder times, challenge lengths and settings are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

Sharing, backup and export

When you share a challenge with a friend, back up your habits, or export them to a spreadsheet, the app puts that information into a message or file and hands it to the app you pick (for example SMS, WhatsApp, email or a cloud drive). That app's own privacy policy then applies. Nothing is sent unless you choose to share it. Challenge and backup codes are compressed, not encrypted, so anyone with the message can read it.

Reminders and the home-screen widget

If you turn on reminders, Android schedules them on your phone. If you allow "Alarms & reminders", they arrive on the minute. The home-screen widget reads today's habits from the app's storage on your phone. No reminder or widget content leaves your device.

Advertising

The app is free and shows ads (banner, full-screen and app-open ads, and optional rewarded ads) using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location and app interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the menu ("Privacy settings").

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Permissions

Internet and network state: to load ads. Notifications, alarms & reminders and run at startup: to deliver the reminders you turn on, on time and after the phone restarts. Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above. The app does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

Because your habits are stored only on your device, deleting a habit in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app removes them. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy changes with it and the date at the top is updated.
