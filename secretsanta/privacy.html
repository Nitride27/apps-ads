01

Information you enter

Names, wishlists, budgets, dates and notes you type into the app are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information. We do not collect your name, email address, contacts or location.

02

Match, wishlist and backup codes

To send someone their match, ask for wishlists or move to a new phone, the app turns the details into a text code, and you choose where it goes. The app hands the text to the app you pick (for example SMS, WhatsApp or email), and that app's own privacy policy then applies. Match codes are scrambled so the name is not readable at a glance, but they are not encrypted, so share them only with the person they are for.

03

Reminders

If you turn on gift reminders, they are scheduled on your phone by Android. No reminder content leaves your device.

04

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and app interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the menu ("Privacy settings").

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

05

Permissions

Internet and network state: to load ads. Notifications, run at startup and wake lock: to deliver the reminders you turn on, including after the phone restarts. Vibrate: so a reminder can buzz. Advertising ID: used by Google AdMob as described above. Google's ads library also declares a background-work permission for its own tasks. The app does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone, location or files.

06

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

07

Data deletion

Because your event data is stored only on your device, deleting an event in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app removes it. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to delete on our side.

08

Security

Your data stays in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

09

Changes

If the way the app handles data changes, this policy changes with it and the date at the top is updated.
