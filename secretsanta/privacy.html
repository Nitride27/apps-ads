Privacy Policy – Secret Santa

Last updated: 8 November 2026

Samridha Shrestha ("we") built the Secret Santa app ("the app"). This policy explains what information is handled when you use it.

Information you enter

Names, wishlists, budgets, dates and notes you type into the app are stored only on your device. We do not run servers that receive this information. When you choose to share a match, the app hands the text to the messaging app you pick (for example SMS, WhatsApp or email); that app's own privacy policy then applies.

Advertising

The app shows ads using Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect and process information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, and app interaction data. Where required by law (for example in the EEA, UK and some US states) the app asks for your consent first, and you can change your choice at any time from the menu ("Privacy settings").

Learn how Google uses data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads, or Settings → Privacy → Ads).

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion

Because your event data is stored only on your device, deleting an event in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling the app removes it.
