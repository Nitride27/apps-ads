Office Secret Santa

Collect the names, set a budget of $10 to $25 and a date, draw, and send each colleague a private link through Slack, Teams, email or WhatsApp. Nobody signs up or shares an email address. For teams who'd rather not use phones, print the reveal slips and hand them out.

Step by step

  1. Agree the basics. A budget, the exchange date, and whether it's opt-in. It should be.
  2. Add the names. Paste them straight from a sign-up message.
  3. Draw and send. Send each person their link in a direct message, or print fold-over slips, six to a page.
  4. Collect wishlists. Optional: one invite gets everyone's likes, sizes and no-gos back to you.

Keep it comfortable

  • Make it opt-in, and never ask why someone sat out.
  • Keep the budget low and fixed. Say what to do if it's a stretch for anyone.
  • Ask for a wishlist, or at least a few likes, so nobody gets a gift that misses badly.
  • Turn on "I'm playing too" if the organizer is joining in.

Remote and hybrid teams

Links work on any phone or laptop, so remote colleagues get their match the same way. Agree whether gifts are posted, delivered or opened on a video call, and set a shipping deadline as the date.

Questions

What is a good budget for office Secret Santa?

$10 to $25 is common. Lower is fine; what matters is that everyone sticks to the same number.

Do colleagues need to install an app?

No. The link opens in the browser. Only the organizer needs the app, or they can use the web generator.
