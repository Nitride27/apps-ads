Kris Kringle by any name

Kris Kringle is the same gift exchange as Secret Santa: everyone draws one name and buys that person a gift. It goes by Kris Kindle in Ireland, Monito Monita in the Philippines, Chris Mom Chris Child in India, Wichteln in Germany and amigo secreto or amigo invisible in Spanish and Portuguese. The app draws names for any of them.

Monito Monita weeks

Monito Monita often runs for weeks, with a theme each week, such as something soft or something sweet, before the final gift. Set the final date in the app and use the note to list the themes.

Questions

Is Kris Kringle the same as Secret Santa?

Yes. The names differ by country, but the draw and the rules are the same.
