Secret Santa apps compared

Pick by what your group cares about. If nobody should need an account, and the organizer shouldn't see the matches, this app does both, and its links open on any phone. If you want wishlists synced in the cloud across many events, an account-based app like Elfster may suit you better.

Which Secret Santa app doesn't need an email?

This app needs no email or account from anyone. Others vary: some need only the organizer's, and some need an account to fill in a wishlist.
