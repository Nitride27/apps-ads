White Elephant rules

Everyone brings one wrapped gift and draws a number. On your turn, open a new gift or steal one that's already open. Someone who loses a gift opens a new one or steals another, but can't steal straight back. A gift is frozen after its third steal, and player one gets a final chance to swap at the end.

Step by step

  1. Draw numbers. Shuffle the turn order. The app does this for you.
  2. Take turns. Open a wrapped gift, or steal an opened one.
  3. Handle steals. The person robbed picks again straight away, but not the gift just taken from them.
  4. Freeze gifts. After three steals a gift stays put.
  5. Final swap. Player one, who never got to steal, may swap with anyone at the end.

Common house rules

  • Set a budget, often $20 to $30, and say if gag gifts are welcome.
  • Limit steals per turn so the game doesn't stall.
  • Some groups skip the final swap. Decide before you start.

White Elephant vs Secret Santa

In Secret Santa you buy for one person you drew. In White Elephant, also called Yankee Swap or Dirty Santa, nobody knows who ends up with their gift, and stealing is the fun.

The Secret Santa app runs both. White Elephant mode keeps the turn order, counts steals per gift, and has an undo for the last tap.

Questions

How many times can a gift be stolen in White Elephant?

Most groups freeze a gift after three steals. The app counts them for you.

Can you steal back a gift that was just stolen from you?

Usually not on the same turn. You open a new gift or steal a different one.
